# Google Form setup blueprint

Title: PES × Tracxn — The Discovery Challenge

Description: PES Library Week · 14–21 November 2026. Research each question using your institution's Tracxn account. Follow the navigation hints, enter your answers, and review before submitting. Platform figures can change; the organiser will provide the reference date and final rules.

Use a muted green theme and matching header. Google Forms controls styling inside the embed. Recommended structure: Participant details, Questions 1–5, Questions 6–10. Do not add a timer or promise prizes unless the organiser confirms them.

## Participant details — proposed fields

- Full name: short answer.
- University email: short answer with email validation, or the Forms email collection setting. Choose whether to verify sign-in or accept typed addresses; typed addresses do not prove identity.
- Student ID: short answer.
- Programme / department: short answer.

Confirm required fields and participant data notice with PES. Use one field per sub-answer below. Numeric fields should accept nonnegative whole numbers where appropriate; funding amounts should include currency and units.

## Questions from the supplied PDF

These are draft prompts, not a verified answer key. The PDF's October 2026 news claims must be checked before they are repeated publicly. Add the validated headline, reference date, and exact Tracxn URL in each question description.

1. **Microsoft acquisitions.** How many companies has Microsoft acquired in the last 2 years? How many of them are AI companies? Fields: total acquisition count; AI acquisition count. Hint: Microsoft profile → Acquisitions → last 24 months. Set an explicit date range and AI classification rule.
2. **Miro funding.** What was Miro's last funding round, and who were its top 3 investors? Fields: latest round details; investor 1; investor 2; investor 3. Hint: Miro profile → Funding rounds / Investors. Define “top” before launch; do not assume rank by invested capital is available.
3. **Bending Spoons acquisitions.** How many acquisitions has Bending Spoons made? Name any 3. Fields: total count; acquisition 1; acquisition 2; acquisition 3. Hint: Bending Spoons profile → Acquisitions.
4. **Classiq.** How much had Classiq raised in total? Name 2 competitors. Fields: total funding with currency; competitor 1; competitor 2. Hint: Classiq profile → Funding / Competitors.
5. **Blume Ventures healthtech portfolio.** Name 3 other healthtech startups in Blume Ventures' portfolio. Fields: company 1; company 2; company 3. Hint: Blume Ventures investor profile → Portfolio → Healthtech. The original context concerns Cent; explicitly state which company must be excluded in the published question.
6. **NVIDIA in India.** How many Indian startups has NVIDIA invested in? Name 2. Fields: count; startup 1; startup 2. Hint: NVIDIA investor profile → Portfolio → India. Confirm the relevant investor entity and definition of India.
7. **Peak XV sectors.** Which 3 sectors does Peak XV invest in the most? Fields: sector 1; sector 2; sector 3. Hint: Peak XV investor profile → Portfolio by sector. Specify ranking by portfolio company count and how ties are handled, if that is the approved measure.
8. **Tractian.** When was Tractian founded? How many years did it take to become a unicorn? Fields: founding year; first unicorn year; elapsed years. Hint: Tractian profile → Overview / Funding rounds. Verify the event and specify calendar-year subtraction if exact dates are unavailable.
9. **Indian gaming startups.** Name 3 other Indian gaming startups and their latest funding round. Use 3 paragraph fields, each asking for company name, latest round, date and amount if required. Hint: Gaming sector → India → latest funding. The original context concerns Matiks; explicitly exclude Matiks. Define acceptable funding-round details.
10. **Instinct.** Who led the round? Compare Instinct's total funding with 2 competitors. Fields: round lead investor(s); Instinct total funding; competitor 1 and funding; competitor 2 and funding; brief comparison. Hint: Instinct profile → Funding rounds / Competitors. The supplied context describes a Series C; verify the exact company and round before publishing. Use one currency and a common reference date.

## Evaluation and release

Recommend manual grading for these multipart research answers. Keep the marking guide private. If enabling Google Forms quiz mode, review point allocation and release grades only after review; do not expose answers during the event by accident.

Suggested confirmation: “Thank you. Your PES × Tracxn Discovery Challenge response has been recorded.” Do not promise an email receipt unless enabled and tested. Confirm the event opening and closing times in IST and manage response collection in Forms.
